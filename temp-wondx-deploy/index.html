Our Solutions

Three Systems. One Connected Property.

GRMS — Guest Room Management

A centralized dashboard controlling lighting, HVAC, curtains and do-not-disturb across every room, synced with your PMS.

• Centralized room dashboard • HVAC & lighting scenes • PMS & key-card integration • Real-time occupancy sensing

Self Check-In Kiosks

Guests verify ID, settle payment and collect a digital or physical key without ever visiting the front desk.

• ID & passport scanning • Instant key issuance • Multi-language interface • Payment & folio capture

Smart Home Automation

The same automation extended to long-stay and residential units — voice, app control and energy-saving schedules.

• Mobile app & voice control • Scene presets (Away / Sleep / Home) • Energy-saving scheduling • Works with existing smart devices
